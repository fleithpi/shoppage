What is a WordPress page?

A WordPress Page is a post type designed to display static, evergreen content—that is, important information that doesn’t change frequently.
Unlike “Posts” (which are traditional blog articles), Pages have the following characteristics:
Common use: They are ideal for creating the fixed structure of your website, such as the “About Us,” “Contact,” “Services,” or “Privacy Policy” sections.
No chronological order: They are not published based on a date and do not appear in the update feed or on the blog’s homepage.
Hierarchy: Pages can be organized into levels. You can have a “Parent Page” (e.g., Services) and several “Subpages” subordinate to it (e.g., Web Development, Graphic Design).
No categories or tags: Since they are structural elements and not routine news items, they do not use the categorization system used for posts.

Uma Página no WordPress é um formato de publicação projetado para exibir conteúdo estático e atemporal — ou seja, informações importantes que não mudam com frequência.
